# 4.02 EC2 Pricing Models

## Topics

- On-Demand – pay per second/hour, no commitment
- Reserved Instances – 1 or 3 year commitment, Standard vs. Convertible
- Savings Plans – Compute Savings Plans vs. EC2 Instance Savings Plans
- Spot Instances – up to 90% discount, can be interrupted
- Dedicated Hosts vs. Dedicated Instances
- Capacity Reservations
- Pricing fundamentals: pay for compute, storage and outbound data transfer (inbound is free)
- AWS Free Tier – always free, 12 months free, trials
